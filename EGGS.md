# Easter eggs 🥚

A maintainer's checklist of the deliberate easter eggs hidden in NUMERUS. These are hints, not spoilers: go and find them.

The eggs follow a few house rules:
- Every egg sits at the end of a notebook (usually inside a collapsed `<details>` note marked "🥚"). Its code cell is hidden, tagged `easter-egg`, and starts with a comment saying it is deliberate.
- Standard library only, no `input()`, no network, under a second, and no randomness that could fail.
- None of them touch the teaching content, and "Run All" stays error- and warning-free.
